Study information and consent

About this study

Study title: [Insert faculty-approved study title]

This research asks neonatal-care professionals to observe a computer-generated newborn breathing simulation and record the assessment they would make in the situation presented. You will complete eight short observations. Each observation includes a simulated eight-minute breathing-rate history followed by 30 seconds of live animation.

What participation involves

  • Participation is voluntary and should take approximately 10–15 minutes.
  • You may change your green, yellow, or red assessment during the live observation.
  • You will be asked about confidence, realism, and any additional information you would need.
  • You may close the page before submitting. Completed responses on this device may be retained as an unfinished local draft until you resume or clear browser data.

Privacy and risks

The survey does not ask for your name, email address, staff number, patient details, or other direct identifiers. A random session code is generated to group the eight scenario responses into one submission. Please do not enter identifying information in free-text comments.
